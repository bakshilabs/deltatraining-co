      Questions

      Questions schools ask.

      Do our instructors need to know about AI?

      No. A non-specialist instructor must be able to run it, and that's a design rule. Every session has a minute-by-minute plan with the words to say, and the training ends with each instructor running a complete lesson.

      Will it work behind our school firewall?

      Yes. There is no open AI model and no chat. The example AI runs offline on the device, so nothing needs to reach an outside service, and we help schools set it up on their own devices and networks.

      What happens to children's data?

      There is no account to create, and what children make stays on the device. A shared build travels inside its link, so no server is needed.

      Is this a coding course?

      No. It's maths and science, learned by building with AI. Children practise saying exactly what they mean, predicting, testing fairly and deciding from evidence.

      How do we schedule it?

      Run it as a half-term block or as six weekly one-hour sessions. Each session stands alone in the timetable, and the build carries on to the next one.

      How much does it cost?

      We agree pricing and terms with each partner once we understand what you need. Book a call and we'll talk it through.
