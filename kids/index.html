The four builds

Children learn the same skills in different builds.

Each child picks a build that interests them, and the whole class learns the same skill at the same time. In every build, the AI gets something wrong on purpose, so children learn to catch it.

Space

Planet Builder

Gravity and forces, halving and doubling. Children finish with a Planet Passport for the planet they built.

The AI's mistake“As high as possible” sets gravity to zero, so the astronaut floats away.

Sport

Kick Lab

Forces and angles. Children finish with a Free-Kick Challenge that friends can try.

The AI's mistakeIt reads “high” as a 70° kick, and the ball lands short of the goal.

Music

Beat Lab

Sound and pitch, fractions and beats per minute. Children finish with a named track of their own.

The AI's mistake“Make the melody higher” comes out louder, not higher in pitch.

The planet

Power Town

Energy, percentages and line graphs. Children finish with a Town Energy Plan that keeps the lights on.

The AI's mistake“Clean energy” means solar only, so the town loses power at night.

FAQ

Questions schools ask.

Do instructors need to know about AI?

No. A non-specialist instructor must be able to run it. Every session plan includes timing, instructor prompts and common problems, and training ends with each instructor running a complete lesson.

Will it work behind our firewall?

Yes. There is no open AI model and no chat. The example AI runs offline on the device, so nothing needs to reach an outside service.

What happens to children's data?

There is no account to create, and what children make stays on the device. A shared build travels inside its link, so no server is needed.

Is this a coding course?

No. It's maths and science, learned by building with AI. Children practise saying exactly what they mean, predicting, testing fairly and deciding from evidence.

How do we schedule it?

Run it as a half-term block or as six weekly one-hour sessions, for around 15 children. Each child keeps the build they chose for all six hours.

What equipment do we need?

Laptops with a current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. A simpler 3D mode helps older machines, and every number still shows without 3D.

Is the unit finished?

No. The structure is an example to shape together. We start by listening, then co-design and pilot it with you before anything scales.

How much does it cost?

We agree pricing and terms with each partner once we understand what you need. Book a call and we'll talk it through.
